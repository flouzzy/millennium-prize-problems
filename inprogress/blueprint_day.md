### [EN] Strategic Blueprint

His research demonstrates finiteness results for K-trivial varieties and establishes, through the Global Torelli Theorem for polarized K3 surfaces, sufficient conditions for the isomorphism of fibers via Hodge theory.

The previous framework focused on a strict motivic fibration approach collided with Krein Space obstructions (documented impasse: `impasses/impasse_fibration_motivique.md`). Similarly, the hope for a purely spectral constraint via density bounds failed against formally forbidden resonant accumulations (`impasses/impasse_resonance_geodesique.md`), as absolute asymmetry paths violated our compactness condition.

**The Paradigm Shift:**

Rather than analytically constraining a shift $\delta > 0$, we will interpret the potential asymmetry of a non-trivial zero as the existence of birationally equivalent but non-isomorphic fibers within a projective family of polarized K3 surfaces associated with the spectrum of the zeta function. The application of the Global Torelli Theorem will enforce that these fibers must be rigorously isomorphic, thus forcing the collapse of any dimensional anomaly. The geometric constraint of rigidity will inevitably constrain $\delta$ to vanish, freezing the alignment on the critical axis $\Re(s) = 1/2$.
