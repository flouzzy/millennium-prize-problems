# Blueprint of the Day: Lemma 19

Based on recent arXiv publications:

- The spectral gap of the Hamiltonian in non-Archimedean geometries.
- Cohomological obstructions to absolute convergence in infinite dimensions.

The strategy for Lemma 19 is to demonstrate that the spectral gap $\Delta$ is strictly bounded below by applying a localized Gribov-Zwanziger deformation to the non-Archimedean Laplacian $\Delta_{NA}$, thus controlling the zeros.
