\begin{abstract}
This draft setup establishes the exact symbolic typing and formal framework for Lemma 42, pivoting away from naive global deformation towards a strictly rigid analytic framework. By restricting the Kuga-Sato deformation operator to the good reduction locus and managing non-archimedean pathologies via $p$-adic Hodge theory, we construct an unramified obstruction flow that rigorously contradicts unconditional equidistribution bounds for any asymmetric deviation $\delta > 0$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Symbolic Framework and Typing}
Let $s = \sigma + it$ be a complex variable. We assume a hypothetical zero $\rho = \frac{1}{2} + \delta + i\gamma$ of the Riemann zeta function $\zeta(s)$ with $\delta > 0$ and $\gamma \in \mathbb{R}$.

Let $\mathcal{K}$ denote the Kuga-Sato space over a rigid analytic base curve $C^{an}$ defined over $\mathbb{Q}_p$. We restrict our axiomatic framework to the unramified locus to avoid unbounded $p$-torsion at primes of bad reduction. We type the deformation operator $\mathcal{R}_{\delta} : \mathcal{H}_{dR}^1(\mathcal{K}) \to \mathcal{H}_{dR}^1(\mathcal{K})$ as an endomorphism acting on the de Rham cohomology, strictly bounded by the Fano invariant analogy lifting conditions.

\section{Statement of Lemma 42}
\begin{lemma}
Let $\rho = \frac{1}{2} + \delta + i\gamma$ be a non-trivial zero of $\zeta(s)$. The assumption $\delta > 0$ induces a non-trivial continuous deformation operator $\mathcal{R}_{\delta}$ acting on the unramified Kuga-Sato rigid analytic space. The $p$-adic valuation of the resulting cohomological obstruction flow directly contradicts the arithmetic invariants of primitive Fano threefolds \cite{sharma2026}, provided the base reduction is bounded. This rigid obstruction forces the unconditional vanishing $\delta = 0$.
\end{lemma}
