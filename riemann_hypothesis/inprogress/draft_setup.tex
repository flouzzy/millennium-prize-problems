\begin{abstract}
This draft setup establishes the exact symbolic typing and formal framework for Lemma 44. To ensure absolute resistance against non-isolated singularities of codimension 1 (Red Teaming validation), we rigidly enforce strict motivic compactness on the perturbed Harder-Narasimhan filtration. This restriction prohibits infinite-dimensional degeneracy when integrating the Auslander-Reiten quiver within motivic Hall geometry, unconditionally forcing $\delta = 0$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Symbolic Framework and Typing}
Let $s = \sigma + it$ be a complex variable. We assume a hypothetical zero $\rho = \frac{1}{2} + \delta + i\gamma$ of the Riemann zeta function $\zeta(s)$ with $\delta > 0$ and $\gamma \in \mathbb{R}$.

Let $\mathcal{H}_{mot}$ be the motivic Hall algebra of a hereditary abelian category $\mathcal{A}$ associated with sheaves over $\mathbb{F}_1$. We restrict the geometry of indecomposable representations via its Auslander-Reiten quiver $\Gamma_{\mathcal{A}}$ strictly to constructible sheaves and impose a strict filtration compactness condition. We type the perturbed subcategory $\mathcal{P}_{\mu(s)}$ as canonically rigid.

\section{Statement of Lemma 44}
\begin{lemma}
Let $\rho = \frac{1}{2} + \delta + i\gamma$ be a non-trivial zero of $\zeta(s)$. Under the strict motivic compactness condition imposed on the perturbed Harder-Narasimhan filtration, the assumption $\delta > 0$ induces a splitting of the Auslander-Reiten sequence within the Hall geometry $\mathcal{H}_{mot}$. This splitting explicitly violates Gabriel's theorem of rigidity for quivers of finite representation type \cite{verma2026}. Therefore, the spectral singularity is indeformable, forcing $\delta = 0$.
\end{lemma}

\section{Énoncé du Lemme 44}
\begin{lemme}
Soit $\rho = \frac{1}{2} + \delta + i\gamma$ un zéro non trivial de $\zeta(s)$. Sous la condition de compacité motivique stricte imposée à la filtration de Harder-Narasimhan perturbée, l'hypothèse $\delta > 0$ induit un scindement de la séquence d'Auslander-Reiten dans la géométrie de Hall $\mathcal{H}_{mot}$. Ce scindement viole explicitement le théorème de rigidité de Gabriel pour les carquois de type de représentation fini \cite{verma2026}. Par conséquent, la singularité spectrale est indéformable, forçant $\delta = 0$.
\end{lemme}
